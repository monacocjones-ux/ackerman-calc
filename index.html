Rules of Engagement

  • 01 Wait for their counter. Never move to the next offer on your own. Make the offer, then stop talking — silence is pressure, and it's their turn to spend it.
  • 02 Say no without saying "no." Lead with tactical empathy — "I hear you, and I know this isn't what you wanted" — before you decline. A calibrated "how am I supposed to do that?" often does the work for you.
  • 03 Shrink your increases. 65 → 85 → 95 → 100 is deliberate — each jump gets smaller, signaling you're closing in on your true ceiling, not just splitting differences.
  • 04 Land on an odd number. Your final figure should look calculated, not rounded — a precise number reads as your true limit, not a negotiating position.
  • 05 Attach a non-monetary item to the final offer. Throw in something that costs you little but signals extra generosity — it reframes the final ask as a bigger concession without moving the price.
